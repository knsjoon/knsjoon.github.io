Geometric Exploitation for Indoor Panoramic Semantic Segmentation

A geometry-aware approach to semantic understanding of indoor 360° panoramic images.

Authors
Duc Cao Dinh, Seok Joon Kim, and Kyusung Cho
Venue
Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 37
Year
2024

The research

Using indoor geometry to improve panoramic scene understanding.

The method separates planar regions such as floors and ceilings from other scene elements, then uses the geometry of each group to guide semantic segmentation.

Dense depth estimation helps optimize floor and ceiling regions. A transformer-based context module and high-resolution branch aggregate multiple geometric representations while preserving detail in the predicted masks. The approach was evaluated on real-world and synthetic indoor panoramic datasets.

01

Geometry-aware decomposition

Indoor panoramic regions are grouped according to their spatial and sampling properties.

02

Depth-guided learning

Joint dense depth estimation provides additional geometric guidance for planar regions.

03

Hybrid decoder

A transformer context module combines geometric representations with a high-resolution prediction branch.
