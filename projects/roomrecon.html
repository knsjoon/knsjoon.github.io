RoomRecon: High-Quality Textured Room Layout Reconstruction

An interactive mobile pipeline for creating complete, editable, and realistic 3D replicas of indoor spaces.

Authors
Seok Joon Kim, Dinh Duc Cao, Federica Spinola, Se Jin Lee, Kyu Sung Cho
Topics
Mobile AR, RGB-D, 3D reconstruction, texturing
DOI
10.1109/ISMAR62088.2024.00069

The research

Better room models, directly on mobile.

RoomRecon reconstructs permanent room elements—walls, floors, and ceilings—then uses guided image capture and generative refinement to produce high-quality textures.

The system is designed around the practical limitations of mobile capture: occlusion, camera drift, blurred images, lighting inconsistency, and the difficulty of updating static scene scans. Its two-phase workflow produces customizable room layouts while keeping computation efficient.

01

Interactive mobile framework

A real-time scanning and texturing pipeline that efficiently generates complete room models.

02

AR-guided texture capture

A divide-and-conquer strategy helps users capture a small set of high-quality images for sharper surface textures.

03

Plane2Image refinement

Textured planes become editable 2D images, enabling conventional processing and diffusion-based inpainting.
